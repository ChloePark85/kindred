# Partner photos

Each AI partner needs a profile photo at `public/partners/{id}.jpg` (portrait, 3:4, at least 900×1200). Until a file exists, the app shows the partner's gradient with their initial.

Generate them in Midjourney or ChatGPT with the prompts below, then save each file under the exact name shown. Midjourney: append ` --ar 3:4 --style raw`. ChatGPT: ask for a portrait (3:4) image.

All partners are fictional AI characters. Don't use photos of real people.
